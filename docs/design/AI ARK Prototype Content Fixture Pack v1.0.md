- Northstar Studio;
- Mina Chen;
- Daniel Park.

These names must not be represented as real public companies, creators, or repositories.

### 2.2 Prototype metrics

All scores, rankings, stars, reviews, dates, and verification findings are synthetic prototype data.

### 2.3 Consistency

The same:

- Resource name;
- creator;
- version;
- Quality Score;
- verification level;
- regional status;

must remain consistent across all screens.

### 2.4 Public and internal copy

Text marked:

> **Public copy**

appears in the clickable prototype.

Text marked:

> **Internal annotation**

is for the designer or researcher and should not appear to test participants.

### 2.5 Sensitive product claims

Avoid public prototype copy using:

- safe;
- guaranteed;
- fully secure;
- works everywhere;
- best in all cases;
- official certification.

Use scoped language.
